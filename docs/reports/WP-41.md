# WP-41 report: inner world look

## Done

- Big dark shape found. It was not a new mesh. It was `root_fear_secondary` (and at some spots `root_background_01`). The camera sits inside or next to these roots at the package spot (camera near x 1.6, y 3.2, z -8.3). The camera fade (WP-36) swapped the root to a clone with `visibility` 0.06. But the glTF material is marked OPAQUE (`transparencyMode` 0). A PBR material with that mode ignores `mesh.visibility`. So the fade never worked for the tree. The player saw the inside of the root at full strength.
- Fix: in `src/world/inner/treeGlow.ts` every tree material gets `transparencyMode = null`. Then visibility below 1 blends again and the fade works. Proof: `docs/evidence/wp-41/*package*` before and after. No geometry was moved. Roots are still the same, so the fade still has to trigger (WP-42 owns it).
- Backdrop (`src/presentation/innerBackdrop.ts`, 3 draw calls): nebula and star dome (one shader, violet, blue, gold), 9 far floating islands in one merged mesh (rock, grass, glowing crystals), 6 crossed light shafts in one mesh. No collider. No light.
- Waterfalls (`src/presentation/innerWaterfalls.ts`, 1 draw call + 1 particle system): 4 ribbons from far islands. Scrolling UV shader, `precision highp float`, soft alpha edges. 14 mist billboards at the feet (7 on low).
- Floor (`src/presentation/innerFloor.ts`, 4 draw calls): rock skirt under the disk (the disk now floats), 46 rim rocks with root tips at the edge, crystal groups and glowing plants (one mesh, tagged `metadata.glow`), soft light pools, one gold-blue beam at the tree foot. The walk area is clear. Crystals on the camera side (south) are low.
- Kit crystals (`src/world/inner/crystalGlow.ts`): own material copy, emissive, glow tag.
- Tree (`treeGlow.ts`): root meshes carry the glow tag. Gold roots use a flat gold emissive that follows `traits.attachment` (state logic is the same). Fear roots and the rest glow cool blue.
- Beetle (`src/creatures/beetle.ts`): meshes carry the glow tag. Attached rules are unchanged. Gold glow still follows the attachment trait.
- Water (`src/presentation/sourceWater.ts`): inner water is brighter cyan and a little more solid. The outer water is the same as before (a `step` on the `glow` uniform). Inner water carries the glow tag. It still starts at the trunk foot.
- Story spot discs (package, beetle, return) are now see-through tinted discs, not pale ovals.

## For WP-42 (light, fog, camera, glow)

- Camera fade: the clone of a root should work now. Set `SEE_THROUGH` as you like. If you clone materials for the fade, keep `transparencyMode` null or set it to blend for the clone.
- Glow tag: `metadata.glow = true` on `inner-crystals-plants`, `inner-waterfalls` (shader material), `SourceWater` (shader material), tree root meshes (`root_*`), kit crystal meshes, beetle meshes. Shader materials have no emissive colour. Check that the glow layer shows them. If not, skip them.
- `atmosphere.ts` `coolFill` overwrites the emissive of `Rock-` and `InnerPlatform-` meshes with (.2,.36,.52). The rim and island kit rocks still look near black in the south view. Suggest (.28,.46,.66) or a lighter albedo.
- Suggested inner values: fog start 18, end 85 now. I suggest fog colour (.08,.17,.36) (more violet), start 26, end 110 so the far islands and waterfalls show. Sun intensity 0.6 is fine. Hemisphere ground colour (.3,.75,.95) is strong; (.25,.55,.85) keeps shapes. Exposure 1.7 is fine.
