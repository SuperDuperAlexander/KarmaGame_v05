## The big dark inner shape
- Cause: a root between camera and player. The blocker fade left it at alpha 0.06. That draws a large dark (or pale) flat shape over the player. The tree materials were also opaque (found by WP-41), so alpha did nothing and the clone could stay solid.
- Test: with the clone switched off, the shape was gone. At alpha 0.06 it was dark. At 0 it was clean.
- Fix (`src/camera/blockerFade.ts`): a blocker fades to 0. Under 0.02 the mesh or clone switches off. It comes back when the view is clear. Works with opaque and blended materials.
