# WP-36 report: speed, view blockers and load size

## Done

- Far detail (spec 15.2). New `src/assets/lod.ts`. At load, each static kit mesh gets one simpler copy. It comes from edge collapse with quadric error on the welded shape. Seam vertices move together, so texture seams stay closed. UV and normals stay. It costs no download. The level swaps by distance on the instances. Houses switch at 38 m (40% of triangles). Walls at 34 m, gate at 46 m, stalls at 30 m, props at 26 m, plants at 30 m. Small props and plants also hide far away (75 to 110 m). The merchant hides at 52 m. Nothing in the walk area is closer than 26 m, so the path has no popping.
- Why not a build step: `meshoptimizer` is not in `package.json`. The lead owns that file. Grid clustering and the Babylon simplifier both broke the texture atlas, so I wrote the collapse myself. Build time is 263 ms in total on a desktop CPU, once, behind the loading screen.
- View blockers (spec 11.2). New `src/camera/blockerFade.ts`, called from the follow camera. A cone of 11 rays runs from the camera to the player, plus 6 short rays around the camera. A tagged mesh that the rays hit fades to 20% and comes back when clear. Tags: all houses, the outer tree parts, all inner tree parts. The asset service sets the tag (`metadata.viewFade`). An instance cannot hold its own alpha, so a faded instance swaps to a clone for the time it is not solid. No extra cost when nothing fades. The ray test runs every second frame.
- The dark-blue wall was not the trunk. It was `root_fear_secondary` (and sometimes `root_background_01`). The camera sat inside or next to it. It shows only when the player stands at some spots (camera x 1.5 to 1.6 at the package), so a test run can miss it. After the change it fades. `docs/evidence/wp-36/*inner*` shows the view.
- Freeze (`src/world/freeze.ts`). `freezeStatic` freezes the world matrix and stops bounding box sync for decor, stalls and the inner rocks, platforms and crystals. Tree, leaves, roots, water, beetle and chain are not frozen. Materials are not frozen: the quality menu and the inner glow change them at run time.
- Load size. The market stalls, market props, merchant and both citizens now load after the first frame (they are 27 m or more from the spawn). The inner world already loads only on the first Look Within.
- Quality menu: `High` added in `src/app/app.ts`. Touch devices still start on `medium`.
- Draw calls in placeholder mode: the 8 placeholder houses are merged by material (`mergeStatic`). Gate view 88 to 74. Square view 67 to 53.
- Optional registry fields `lod` and `fade` added to `src/assets/registry/entry.ts`. Defaults live in `src/assets/lod.ts`, so no registry data file changed.
- New test file `tests/perf-lod.spec.ts` (4 tests).

## Open issues

- `src/debug/debug.ts` (not mine): the triangle count uses `getTotalIndices()` of the source mesh. It ignores the far level. Use `(m.sourceMesh??m).getLOD(scene.activeCamera,m.getBoundingInfo().boundingSphere)` and count that mesh (skip null). Then the `?debug` panel shows the real number.
- The faded roots show as a pale blue ghost on a phone-size screen (see `phone-inner-beetle-after.png`). They have a blue glow, and it stays visible at 20%. Lower `SEE_THROUGH` in `blockerFade.ts` if it looks odd.
- Merchant, stalls and citizens appear 1 to 2 s after the first frame. They are 27 m or more from the spawn. A very slow network can show the market empty for a moment.
- The inner scene fetches player, package and chain again. A shared cache across scenes is a bigger change in `src/assets/feature.ts`. Not done.
- Skinned files (player 2.46 MB) are not quantized in `tools/assets/optimize.mjs`. This is the biggest file left at the start.
- New files outside my list: `src/assets/lod.ts`, `src/camera/blockerFade.ts`, `src/world/freeze.ts`. I changed no registry data file and no string.
