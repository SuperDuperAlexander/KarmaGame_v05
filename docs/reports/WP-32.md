## Fixes in `src/assets/feature.ts` (kit parts)
1. Kit files share one vertex buffer. Mesh and sub mesh bounds cover the whole kit. Fix: read bounds from the used indices (`partBounds`).
2. A part keeps its slot offset from the file (x 4 to 60 m). Fix: recentre the part at the origin and put it on the ground when `normalize` is not set.
3. `normalize` on a part used the whole-kit bounds. It now uses the part bounds.
4. Each part instanced the whole kit, then deleted the rest. Fix: `predicate` option on `instantiateModelsToScene`. Only the part and its parents and children are instanced.
