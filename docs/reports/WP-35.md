# WP-35 report: light, ground, water and mood

## Done

- Ground (outer): grass, arrival path, market path and square have painted textures made in code. Path edges fade out softly. The square has paving, a ring and a fading rim. The arrival path now reaches the square (36 m, was 30 m). Colliders did not change.
- Ground (inner): the grey disk is now carved stone with root seams. The seams glow cyan (emissive texture).
- Light: one sun and one hemispheric light. Warm low sun outside. Cool sun inside. The inner hemispheric ground colour is bright cyan, so it lights roots from below. Inner rocks and platforms get a faint blue self light. The non-fear roots, trunk and branches get a flat dark-blue glow. Before, they were black.
- One shadow map. 1024 px on desktop, 512 px on a narrow screen, off on `low`. 11 casters (player, tree parts, gate, merchant, two citizens). Cap is 12. Ground receives only. The sun follows the player.
- Image processing: ACES, contrast 1.1, exposure, colour saturation lift, light vignette. Off on `low` and `?safe`.
- Sky: one gradient dome (1 draw call, no texture) with a soft sun glow. Fog colour matches the horizon. Outer fog starts at 60 m. The path is never fogged out.
- Source Water: curved ribbon with moving UVs and soft alpha edges. Outer: starts under the tree roots and flows west. Inner: from the trunk foot to the south-west. It ends before the fear root feet. Light specks are billboards.
- Tree state: outer leaves shift a little to warm gold with `traits.attachment`. They pulse once when `TREE_DISCOVERED` turns on. Crown particles burst at the same time. A reload shows the steady look from state (no pulse). Inner gold roots start brighter (level 0.6 instead of 0.25) and rise with attachment. Fear roots glow a little cool blue.
- Particles: billboards only. Outer capacity 156 (water 36, motes 70, crown 50). Inner capacity 180 (water 50, motes 90, gold 40). Half the rate on `low`.
- Carry chain: `src/app/sceneManager.ts` now uses `createInnerChain` (real link, placeholder fallback). It is built at 2x and scaled to 0.5 so the pitch fits the short chain.
- Quality presets in `src/presentation/quality.ts`: `low`, `medium`, `high`. Parts register with `onQuality`. Switching is live.
