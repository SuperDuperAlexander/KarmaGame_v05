# WP-40 report: outer world look

## Done
- Sky: painted dome in `src/presentation/sky.ts`. Golden horizon, blue zenith, soft clouds from a code texture. Inner sky is unchanged.
- Far view: one ring mesh (`far-backdrop`) with hills, mountains, castle and town towers. One draw call. No fog, no light.
- Square: new paving (varied stones, warm patches, worn centre, dirt and moss rim). Planter pots with plants (8). Market goods (crates, barrels, baskets) in three groups. Two more lamps by the benches. Banners (6) on poles with a slow sway.
- Colour: kit materials get a warm tint and a weak warm emissive from their own atlas. Houses read warmer. Roofs read more terracotta.
- Lamps: warm flame ball (tagged `metadata.glow=true`) and an additive halo billboard on each lamp. No point light.
- Windows: arched warm lights placed in the real window openings of the four house kinds. One mesh, tagged for glow.
- Tree: leaf tint is warmer and richer. The leaf texture also feeds a gold emissive, so light leaf tips look gold. State pulse and gold shift are kept.
- Grass and flower tufts: thin instances at path edges, wall foot, square rim and market path. Two draw calls. None on walk lines.
- New code: `src/world/outer/look.ts`. Edits: `feature.ts`, `layout.ts`, `paint.ts`, `sky.ts`, `treeMood.ts`.
