# WP-32 decisions

- Part bounds come from the used vertices, not from mesh bounds. Reason: the kit files share one vertex buffer.
- A kit part is recentred at the origin and grounded. The world sets its scale and yaw. Reason: one registry scale cannot fit all parts.
- Houses use row data in `layout.ts`. Reason: rows are easy to test and keep the alleys closed.
- Decor (props, plants, lamps, far houses) is skipped in placeholder mode. Reason: the placeholder run keeps under 80 meshes.
- Old colored boxes return for walls and edges when the kit is missing. Reason: the swap proof still shows a closed city.
- Tree visual sits 2.2 m north of the tree entity, scaled to 16 m. The trunk blocker moves with it and is larger (5 x 5.2 m). Reason: the trunk is 6 m wide. The Look Within spot at z -2.8 must stay clear. Entity, spot and zones keep the plan coordinates.
- Wall blockers are 1.8 m deep (were 1.3). Stall blockers match the stall depth. Reason: the player must not sink into the new meshes. Positions are the same.
- Gate closed state is a wooden fence part plus the invisible blocker. Reason: it reads as closed and costs 700 triangles.
- The waystone stands on a small fountain-base part. Reason: a stone plinth with a ring. Needs a look check.
- Desire object stays a sphere with more emission. Reason: it must stay clear and glinting. The shared material cannot take a tint.
- Citizens are switched off beyond 32 m (on again within 28 m). Reason: 11-13k triangles each. This keeps the phone gate view under budget.
- Hedges use a large `bushMedium`. Reason: fewer instances for the same cover.
