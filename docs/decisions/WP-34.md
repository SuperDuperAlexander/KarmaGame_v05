# WP-34 decisions

- One skeleton and one clip set for all people. One code path animates them.
- Marks are data in `tools/rig/marks/<name>.json`. Auto fit first. Hand fixes go in `params` and `overrides`, and survive a new fit.
- Calibrate on the player mesh. The player marks were placed by a person, so the offset between mark and measure carries over.
- Weights use the player rule, driven by the marks. Arm zone follows the arm axis. Leg zone follows the leg marks.
- Reach, hip drop and foot lift scale with leg length. Speed stays 2.8 and 4.6 m/s in the world. The authored model speed is divided by the registry scale.
- Walk cycle gets short for short legs. We keep speed over natural cadence.
- Leg inward turn comes from the stance angle (0.09 rad for the player).
- Arm lowering is 0.73 of the A-pose arm angle (the player value).
- Hand grips keep the source position, as children of the hand joints.
- Merchant turn of 180 degrees removed. The new mesh faces +Z.
- Files stay `temporary`. No human bend check yet.
