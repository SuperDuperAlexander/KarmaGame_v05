# WP-31 decisions

- Quantize only meshes with no skin. Reason: skins and clips must stay exact. Risk is low and the saving on skinned files is small.
- Quantize normals to 8 bit, positions to 14 bit. Reason: biggest size gain without decoder code. Babylon reads KHR_mesh_quantization.
- WebP quality 85. Reason: textures were already small. Quality matters more than a few KB.
- Prune with `keepLeaves`. Reason: empty named nodes (grips, `linkStart`) are the registry contract.
- Cache key = source hash + `SETTINGS_VERSION` + texture size. Bump `SETTINGS_VERSION` in `tools/assets/optimize.mjs` when the steps change.
- Fail safe: raw copy and one warning when optimize fails or a node name is lost.
- Register Babylon glTF extensions in `src/assets/feature.ts` (one call). Reason: WebP does not load without it. Needs lead review.
- The 15 MB target is not met (16.9 MB without the raw test rig). Further saving needs forbidden tools or art changes.
