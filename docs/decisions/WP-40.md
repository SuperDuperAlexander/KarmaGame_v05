# WP-40 decisions

- The far hills and towers are a ring mesh, not part of the sky dome. The ring stays at world scale and fits the 180 m camera far plane.
- The painted sky is picked by `horizon.r > .5`. This keeps `atmosphere.ts` unchanged. The inner sky keeps its old shader.
- Window lights use measured positions per house kind (`WINDOWS` in `look.ts`). The atlas cannot be read per window. Quads sit 0.25 m inside the wall plane, so the jambs hide the edges.
- Kit materials get a tint and an emissive from the same atlas. No new texture is added. Roofs and plaster share one atlas, so one tint serves both.
- Lamp flames and halos use shared source meshes plus instances. This costs two draw calls for all lamps.
- Tufts use thin instances with alpha test. Two draw calls cover all vegetation edges.
- Two lamps were added to `LAMPS` in `layout.ts`. The layout test still passes.
- Banners use a pivot node and a sine sway. No per-frame allocation.

## Values for WP-42 (not applied by WP-40)
- Sun direction outer: change `(.62,-.5,.5)` to about `(.45,-.5,-.6)`. Light then falls on south faces (gate, wall, houses seen from spawn).
- Outer fog: start 60, end 170 is fine. A slightly warmer fog colour `(.92,.8,.62)` matches the sky horizon.
- Outer exposure: 1.3 is fine with the new emissive lifts. Do not raise it.
- Glow layer: tag names are `lamp-flame`, `window-lights` (all with `metadata.glow=true`). Keep intensity low for the halos, they are already additive.
