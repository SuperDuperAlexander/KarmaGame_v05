# WP-36 decisions

- Far detail is built at load, not in the optimizer. `meshoptimizer` is not installed and the lead owns `package.json`. The Babylon simplifier keeps seams locked, so it removes only 20%. Grid clustering broke the texture atlas.
- I wrote edge collapse with quadric error. It works on the welded shape. All vertices at one place move together. UV and normals stay per vertex. Seams stay closed. Open edges get a strong plane so rims stay.
- Far levels start at 26 m or more. The walk area is closer than that, so nothing pops near the path.
- Levels hang on the source mesh. All instances share them. Draw calls rise by 4 to 6 because far and near instances draw in separate batches.
- Fade needs a clone. An instance cannot hold its own alpha. The clone is made on the first fade. It shows only while the mesh is not solid.
- The dark wall in the inner world was a fear root, not the trunk. The camera sits next to it at some spots. So all inner tree parts may fade.
- The ray test uses `ray.intersectsMesh`. `mesh.intersects` wants a ray in local space. I lost time on this.
- Fade level is 20%. It is one small number in `blockerFade.ts`. The lead can change it.
- No `material.freeze()`. The quality menu and the inner glow change materials at run time.
- Static meshes freeze their world matrix. Tree, leaves, roots, water, beetle and chain stay live.
- Market, stalls, merchant and citizens load after the first frame. The first frame is the first `onAfterRender` of the outer scene. This cuts 4.09 MB from the start.
- Placeholder houses merge by material. Only in placeholder mode. Real houses are instances already.
- `High` is a plain label in `app.ts`. I did not touch `strings.en.ts`.
- Rule tables (`LOD_DEFAULTS`, `FADE_DEFAULTS`) sit in `lod.ts`. A registry entry can override with `lod` or `fade`. This avoids edits in registry data files that I do not own.
