# WP-42 decisions

## D1. Camera aims above and ahead, orbit stays in spec
- Orbit point stays 1.2 m above the player. Distance default 6.2 m. Pitch default 20 degrees (the spec minimum).
- The view aims at a point 2.2 m ahead and 0.8 m above the orbit point.
- Result: more horizon, more crown, the path ahead shows. The wall test still passes.
- Reason: pitch cannot go below 20 degrees (spec 11.2). Aim offset is the only free tool.

## D2. Blocker fade ends at zero and the mesh switches off
- The big dark shape at the inner package came from a faded root. It stayed at a small alpha (0.06) and drew over the player.
- WP-41 found the tree materials were opaque, so alpha did nothing. The clone stayed solid.
- A blocker now fades to 0. Under 0.02 the mesh (or clone) is switched off. It returns when the view is clear.
- This works with opaque and with blended materials.

## D3. Glow layer: tagged meshes only
- One GlowLayer. It includes only meshes with `metadata.glow === true`.
- Shader materials are skipped. They have no emissive colour, so the layer would draw them black and still cost a draw call.
- The layer is created only when at least one mesh is tagged. An empty include list would mean "all meshes".
- Off on `low` and `?safe`. Phone: texture ratio 0.25, blur 16. Desktop medium: 0.35, 24. High: 0.5, 32.
- Intensity is low (0.45 to 0.65). Crystals have a full white emissive colour. Higher values burn them out.

## D4. Character rim is a shader plugin
- A material plugin adds a fresnel rim and a small lift after tone mapping. No outline, no extra mesh, no extra draw call.
- Rim is warm outside and cool cyan inside. Strength is low so skin does not turn orange.

## D5. Light mood
- Outer: low warm sun (from WP-40 request, light travels toward the camera so faces near the spawn are lit), soft blue sky fill, warm ground bounce, warm haze fog, warm highlights and cool shadows in the colour curves.
- Inner: blue-violet ambient, violet ground bounce, gold from particles and emissive parts.
- Values for the inner fog, rock fill and ground colour come from WP-41.

## D6. Particles
- Outer: pollen (80) follows the player, motes (60), crown (50). Inner: specks (60) follow the player, motes (90), gold (40).
- Total capacity 190 each. Limit is 250. `low` halves the emit rate.

## D7. Carry chain
- The carry chain is built in `sceneManager.ts` (not my file). The chain function now has a carry mode.
- Under the player entity it follows the package in the hand and hangs with a real sag to the hip. 9 links (loose) or 6 (tense).
- Links move only. Still one draw call.
