## Tasks

1. Camera framing: the view shows too much floor and cuts the tree crown. Inside spec §11.2 (distance 4.5–6.5 m, target height 1.2 m, pitch 20–55°): use a lower default pitch, slightly more distance, and aim a bit above and ahead of the player, so the horizon, the crown and the path ahead show. Keep the wall test and blocker fade working. Check spawn, gate, square, market, inner package and beetle views.
2. Big dark shape in the inner world at the package spot (`docs/evidence/wp-36/desktop-inner-package-after.png`): the camera sits behind or inside a root or trunk. Make the camera fade or avoid it so it never fills the screen. WP-41 may also move that root. Your fix must work alone.
3. Glow layer (new `glow.ts`): one Babylon GlowLayer that includes only meshes with `metadata.glow === true` (see common rules). Off on `low` and in `?safe`. Reduced texture size on phones. Measure its draw call cost and report it.
4. Light mood: outer golden hour (warm low sun, soft blue shadows, slight warm haze near the horizon, stronger colour grade toward the concept but no crushed blacks). Inner: cool blue-violet ambient with warm gold accents. Keep one sun and one hemispheric light.
5. Characters (new `characterLook.ts`): people read dark against the ground. Add a soft rim or fresnel light and a small brightness lift to player, merchant and citizens, so their silhouette reads at 390×844. No outlines (spec §15.1: no hard black contours).
6. Particles: magic motes and pollen near the tree and lamps outside, light specks inside. At most 250 in view, half on `low`.
7. Carry chain on the player (`src/props/`): it hangs as a few loose links near the leg. Make it a clear, loose chain from the hand-held package. One draw call.
8. Quality presets: `low` = no glow, no shadows, fewer particles; `medium` = small glow; `high` = full. Default on touch: `medium`.
