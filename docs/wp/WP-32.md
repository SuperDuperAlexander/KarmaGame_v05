## Goal

The outer world uses the new models. It looks like a warm, lived-in small city around the Central Tree. Gameplay stays the same.

## Tasks

1. Measure each new file first: bounds, unit size, facing, ground. Use `?viewer` or a small node script with `tools/assets/glb.mjs`.
2. Registry entries (status `temporary`) with `parts` maps. `create(id, scene, parent, part)` loads one kit part.
   - `CentralTreeOuter` → `/assets/core/central_tree_outer.glb`. About 16 m high. Nodes map `leaves`, `trunk`, `roots` for later glow.
   - `CityGate`, `CityWall` → `/assets/city/city_infrastructure.glb` parts (`city_gate`, `wall_straight`, `wall_corner`, `street_lamp`, `bench`, `fountain_base` …). The gate opening must fit 4 m wide.
   - `CityBuilding` → `/assets/city/city_building_kit.glb` parts (`small_house`, `medium_house`, `corner_house`, `tower` …).
   - `MarketStallB` → `/assets/market/market_stall_B.glb`. `MarketProps` → `/assets/market/market_props.glb` parts. `Vegetation` → `/assets/world/vegetation_kit.glb` parts.
   - `Citizen` → `/assets/characters/citizen_female.glb`. `CitizenMale` → `/assets/characters/citizen_male.glb`. Static now. WP-34 adds rigs later.
3. Rebuild `src/world/outer/feature.ts` with these parts. Keep every gameplay position from `docs/PLAN.md` "Layout": spawn, waystone, gate z −26, tree at (0,0), Look Within spot, market x 21..36, merchant, desire object, world edges, killY.
4. Colliders stay invisible boxes (`solid` with no color). Visual meshes are not pickable and do not collide. Keep `cameraBlocker` metadata on blocking boxes.
5. Close the north and west alleys with houses or walls. Frame the square with houses. Put props at the market and along the path. Put vegetation at the edges, never on the walk line.
6. The desire object stays clear and slightly glinting. Use a market prop with a soft warm emissive tint, or keep the sphere. Keep its `interaction` metadata.
7. Two citizens stand on the square with a light code idle sway (like `src/npc/merchant.ts`; put the helper in `src/world/outer/`). No story, no interaction.
8. Repeated parts must reuse one loaded file (the service caches per file). Report draw calls.
