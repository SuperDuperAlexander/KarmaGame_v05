## Goal

Spec §15.1: warm, painterly outer world; cool, glowing inner world. Beauty from light, fog, colour and few particles, not from polygons.

## Tasks

1. Ground. Outer: the square, path and grass are flat single colours. Give them a painterly look: a small procedural or code-made texture (paving on the square, packed earth on the path, grass variation), soft edges between zones. Inner: replace the flat grey disk with a carved stone look with root seams. Keep the collision boxes as they are.
2. Light. Keep one sun and one hemispheric light (spec: 1 dynamic main light, 2 hard). Add one shadow map: 1024 px on desktop, 512 or off on `low`; 12 casters or fewer (player, NPCs, tree, gate). Warm low sun outside. Light the inner roots from below (now they read as a dark ceiling).
3. Image processing: ACES tone mapping, small contrast and saturation lift, light vignette. Turn it off in `?safe`/`Simple` mode.
4. Sky and fog: outer sky gradient (cheap: clear colour plus a large gradient dome or a backdrop), fog that hides the world edge but never the path. Inner: deep blue fog with soft glow.
5. Source Water (spec §9): it must start visibly at or under the tree roots and flow. Narrow mesh, moving UVs, soft alpha edges, small light specks. No fluid simulation. Inner: move or shorten the strip so it does not cut the fear roots in the west.
6. Tree state: outer leaves react softly to state (pulse on `TREE_DISCOVERED`, slight warm gold shift with `traits.attachment`). Use the registry node map `leaves`. Small particles at the crown. Inner: keep the gold attachment roots from `treeGlow.ts`; make them readable.
7. Particles: 250 or fewer in view, half on `low`. Billboards only.
8. Carry chain: `src/app/sceneManager.ts` still uses the code torus chain on the player. Switch it to the real link from `src/props/chain.ts`, with the placeholder fallback.
9. Quality presets in `src/presentation/quality.ts`: `low`/`medium`/`high` switch shadows, particles and image processing live.
10. Every lasting look comes from state, so a reload shows the right mood (AD-9).
