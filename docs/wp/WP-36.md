## Goal

Spec §15.2 and §21: the phone view stays well inside budget, the first load is small, and nothing blocks the view of the player.

## Tasks

1. Desktop spawn and gate views show 285,000 to 296,000 triangles (hard cap 250,000). Phone spawn shows 152,000. Cut far cost: distance culling or a cheap LOD for houses, walls and the tree (glTF-Transform `simplify` in the optimizer to make `_lod1` files, or Babylon `addLODLevel` with a far `null` level for small props). Target: desktop at most 220,000, phone at most 140,000, without visible popping near the path.
2. View blockers (spec §11.2): buildings and the tree trunk may fade softly when they hide the player. In the inner world the trunk shows as a big flat dark-blue wall in front of the camera (see `beetle-desktop-after.png`). Fade it, or keep the camera from going behind it. Use the existing `cameraBlocker` metadata. Keep the camera wall test passing.
3. Freeze what never moves: `freezeWorldMatrix` on static visuals, `material.freeze()` where safe, `doNotSyncBoundingInfo`. Do not freeze anything that WP-35 animates (leaves, roots, water, beetle, chain).
4. Load size (spec §15.2: start download without audio at most 12 MB target, 18 MB hard): measure the bytes the outer world downloads before the first playable frame (Playwright network log). Load inner-world files only on the first Look Within (check if this is already true). Report the numbers.
5. Quality: add `High` to the quality menu in `src/app/app.ts`. Default on touch devices stays `medium` or lower.
6. Draw calls in placeholder mode reach 85 in the desktop city view. Bring it under 80 if a cheap merge is possible; else report why not.
