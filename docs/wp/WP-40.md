## Tasks

1. Sky and far view: a painted sky with a warm golden horizon, soft clouds and blue zenith (gradient dome texture made in code). Add a cheap far backdrop: distant hills or mountains and a castle or tower silhouette on 1 to 3 large billboards or a ring mesh, behind the city. 3 draw calls or fewer.
2. Square: there is too much empty, flat paving. Break it up: a few planters, flower pots, crates or market goods from the kits, a banner pole, two benches with a lamp. Make the paving less grid-like (stone variation, worn centre, dirt at the edges, soft colour patches). Keep a clear walk line to the tree, the market and the gate.
3. Colour: houses, walls and gate read dark and grey. Lift them toward the concept (warm plaster, terracotta roofs) with a material tint or emissive lift. No new textures over 1024 px.
4. Lamps: give lamp heads a warm emissive flame and a soft halo billboard. Tag flames `metadata.glow = true`. Add a few warm window lights on houses (emissive quads, tagged). No point lights.
5. Life: banners or cloth with a slow sway (vertex or node rotation, cheap). Stall canopies in different strong colours like the concept.
6. Tree: the crown must read rich and full from the square and from spawn. Improve leaf colour (warmer, more saturated greens with light gold tips) in `treeMood.ts`. Keep the state pulse and the gold shift.
7. Grass and path edges: add vegetation clumps along the edges as thin instances. No vegetation on the walk line.
